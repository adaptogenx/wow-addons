Extend HMF_TargetingLab to validate reliable one-click casting against specific tracked enemies.

Test scenario: Two identical mobs are feared while the player targets a third enemy.

* Track both mobs by GUID.
* Assign each a stable HUD row and raid marker.
* Provide a clickable Fear button for each mob.
* Display the GUID, current unit token, and marker for each row.
* Switch targets, move around, and allow nameplate indices to change.
* Verify whether clicking each button casts Fear on the intended GUID.
* Log intended GUID versus actual spellcast target GUID.
* Detect incorrect targeting, stale bindings, and unavailable unit tokens.
* Never silently cast on the wrong mob.

Report whether reliable one-click GUID-specific casting is achievable and which secure targeting mechanism supports it.
