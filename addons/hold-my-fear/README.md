Hold My Fear is a lightweight World of Warcraft Forever Warlock addon designed to manage multiple feared enemies and their debuffs during combat. It automatically tracks enemies by GUID, assigns cycling raid markers, and maintains stable GUID-to-marker-to-HUD-position mappings throughout each combat encounter. Its compact, headerless HUD displays one row per enemy, with the raid marker outside the left border, a directional arrow outside the right border, and the mob’s portrait, active DoTs/Curses with countdown overlays, and Fear timer inside. The mob’s name and level appear underneath. The primary goal is one-click recasting of individual debuffs on tracked enemies without manually switching targets. The initial implementation targets solo play, with secure targeting feasibility investigated before building the full addon.
